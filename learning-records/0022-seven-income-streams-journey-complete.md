## Key insight
There are **seven distinct income streams** (local emp, remote emp, freelance/contract, staff aug/BPO, AI annotation/expert eval, education/content, products/indie) — distinct markets with different gates and ceilings, not levels of one ladder; most people stack two. Every number must be read as **order-of-magnitude, verified before deciding** — public figures are biased (self-reported, marketing, lagging). This is Phase 4 (Look back) from L12 applied to careers. The deepest closing idea: **AI-augmented SDLC makes the evaluation skill (L17: Big O, quality attributes, judgment) the premium human skill** — which is exactly what these 22 lessons built.
