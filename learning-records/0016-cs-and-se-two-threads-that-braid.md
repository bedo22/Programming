## Context
The learner asked about "judgment and critical thinking" — the evaluation skill that goes beyond solving a problem to evaluating whether the solution is good. A full reference was created (550 lines, 20 sections, 18 tables) covering the 90-year lineage, the underlying theory, the quality attributes, Big O, trade-offs, engineering principles, and the future.

## Key insight
CS (the science of computation, founded by Turing 1936) and SE (the engineering of software, named at the 1968 NATO conference) are two threads that braid. CS gives the measures (Big O); SE gives the parameters (quality attributes) and the principles (DRY, SOLID, KISS, YAGNI). HackerRank is CS; the React job is SE. Knowing the difference explains why they feel different. The principles (KISS, YAGNI, DRY, SOLID) are the constants that survive every framework, from Knuth (1968) to the React Compiler (2024).
